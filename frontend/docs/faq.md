## 1. About HalaPark

### What is HalaPark?
HalaPark is a smart parking platform that enables users to find, book, and manage parking spaces seamlessly through a digital experience.

### Why should I use HalaPark?
HalaPark helps you save time, reduce parking stress, and access convenient parking locations while offering a fully cashless and user-friendly experience.

### Where does HalaPark operate?
HalaPark currently operates in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

### Who can use HalaPark?
HalaPark is available to individual drivers, businesses, and property owners.

## 2. Booking & Reservations

### Can I book parking in advance?
Yes, you can search and reserve available parking spaces in advance.

### How far in advance can I reserve a space?
Depends on the location and provider. Available slots are shown during booking.

### Can I book parking for another person?
Yes, by entering the correct vehicle details.

### Are recurring or long-term bookings available?
Yes, at selected locations.

### What happens if I arrive earlier or later?
It may affect your booking. Extra charges may apply.

### Is my parking space guaranteed?
Yes, once booking is confirmed.

## 3. Parking Experience

### How does parking work?
Search → Book → Follow access instructions.

### How do I access the parking location?
Via QR code, license plate recognition, or instructions provided.

### How does entry work after booking?
ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) opens the barrier automatically.

### What if I cannot access parking?
Contact support immediately.

### What if someone occupies my spot?
Report via support.

### Are multiple entries allowed?
Yes, within booking validity.

## 4. Payments & Pricing

### How are parking fees calculated?
Based on location, duration, and tariff.

### What payment methods are accepted?
Credit/debit cards.

### Will I receive invoices?
Yes, digital receipts are provided.

### How to download invoices?
1. Go to **Account → Bills**
2. Open **Paid section**
3. Select order → Preview
4. Tap Download

### Can duplicate charges occur?
No, but contact support if it happens.

### What if payment fails?
Booking won’t be confirmed.
